# Effect Caster Tracking Mechanic

## INTENT
To implement effect caster tracking where all effects remember their originator (caster) until effect ends, enabling proper credit assignment and interruption mechanics even after caster death.

## THE RULE / LOGIC
**Effect Caster Tracking Mechanic:**

**Core Principle:**
Every active effect, modifier, or temporary entity must maintain a persistent reference to its originator (the caster) to ensure accurate credit attribution, accountability, and mechanic resolution throughout its entire lifecycle.

**Tracking Architecture:**
- **Originator Identity (CasterID):** A unique identifier linked to the entity that initiated the effect. This reference must remain valid even if the casting entity is removed from the grid or eliminated.
- **Temporal Context (OriginTime):** A timestamp or turn-counter marking exactly when the effect was applied, used for duration calculations and audit trails.
- **Effect Metadata:** Categorization of the effect (e.g., Shield, Poison, Stun, Buff) to determine which credit-earning rules apply.

**Credit Attribution Logic:**
- **Performance-Based Earning:** Credits generated by the effect (e.g., damage mitigated by a shield, health restored by a HoT, or damage dealt by poison) are automatically assigned to the original CasterID.
- **Post-Mortem Accrual:** If the caster dies while their effects are still active, any subsequent credits earned by those effects continue to be attributed to the caster's account/score.
- **Completion Bonuses:** Any bonuses triggered upon the natural expiration of an effect are awarded to the original caster.

**Lifecycle and Interruption Rules:**
- **Persistence:** Effects typically survive the death of their caster unless specifically defined as "Linked" or "Channeled."
- **Dispel/Purge:** When an effect is prematurely removed by an external force, credit accrual stops immediately, but previously earned credits are retained by the caster.
- **Temporary Entities:** Traps, turrets, and area-of-effect zones inherit the CasterID of their creator to handle damage/healing attribution consistently across the entire temporary entity system.
